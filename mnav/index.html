    The three multiples

    Equity premium
    Market cap divided by the bitcoin left for common shareholders once debt and preferred stock at par are paid in BTC terms. Cash is left out, since Strategy earmarks its USD reserve for dividends and interest.
    Enterprise
    Market cap plus debt and preferred, minus the USD reserve and cash, divided by the value of the bitcoin. This was Strategy's headline mNAV until July 2026.
    Basic
    Market cap divided by the value of the bitcoin, with no adjustment for what sits ahead of the common stock.

    Market cap uses basic shares outstanding. The value of the bitcoin is holdings times the BTC price at the 4:00 pm ET close. Debt is principal outstanding and preferred is notional at par, as Strategy reports them (since August 31, 2026 Strategy counts only its convertible notes as debt). Before 2025 the company had no preferred stock and the debt figures come from its filings. The equity premium is blank for parts of 2022, when the debt nearly matched the value of the bitcoin and the ratio stops meaning anything.
