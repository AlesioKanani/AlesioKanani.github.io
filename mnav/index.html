MSTR and ASST mNAV

What the market pays for the bitcoin behind each share of Strategy and Strive, measured three ways. The history refreshes through the day from each company's own figures, and the newest point updates when you open the page.

Strategy (MSTR) Strive (ASST)

The three multiples

Equity premium
Market cap divided by the bitcoin left for common shareholders once debt and preferred stock at par are paid in BTC terms. Cash is left out, since both companies earmark theirs for dividends and interest.
Enterprise
Market cap plus debt and preferred, minus cash and similar assets, divided by the value of the bitcoin.
Basic
Market cap divided by the value of the bitcoin, with no adjustment for what sits ahead of the common stock.

Market cap uses basic shares outstanding. The value of the bitcoin is holdings times the BTC price at the 4:00 pm ET close. Debt is principal outstanding and preferred is notional at par or stated amount.

Strategy

Debt and preferred are as Strategy reports them (since August 31, 2026 it counts only its convertible notes as debt), and cash means its USD reserve plus cash. Before 2025 the company had no preferred stock and the debt figures come from its filings. The equity premium is blank for parts of 2022, when the debt nearly matched the value of the bitcoin and the ratio stops meaning anything.

Strive

The history starts on September 15, 2025, the first trading day after Strive's merger with Asset Entities and its first large bitcoin purchase. Prices and share counts are adjusted for the 1-for-20 reverse split in February 2026, and shares are Class A and Class B plus pre-funded warrants. Preferred is SATA at its $100 stated amount; cash includes the Strategy preferred stock Strive holds. Strive's dashboard reports holdings, shares and cash weekly, while the SATA count and the months before April 2026 come from its SEC filings, so days between filings are interpolated. The debt is the Semler Scientific notes and loan taken on in the January 2026 merger, retired by May 2026.

Figures from Strategy's and Strive's public dashboard APIs (the data behind strategy.com and strive.com/treasury) and the companies' SEC filings. Your browser requests the newest bitcoin price and MSTR quote from Strategy's API; Strive's quote is refreshed with the history.
